# Governance

## Status

This is a provisional founding policy. No council membership is implied by repository access. Founding appointments and ratification must be recorded publicly before a stable balance release.

## Maintainers are the rules council

The council triages issues, evaluates evidence, drafts and edits changes, reviews pull requests, and publishes balance patch documents. There is no separate technical writing body.

Playtesters contribute evidence. Organisers decide whether their events adopt a release. Repository owners administer access and recovery; administrative access alone does not confer a rules vote.

## Membership

The council consists of three maintainers covering event organisation, competitive and casual play, several factions, precise rules writing and evidence analysis. Avoid a majority from one club.

Use a public nomination issue. Candidates state experience, availability and relevant interests. Founding organisers and contributors discuss nominations; the founder records the initial appointments and reasons in that issue and updates MAINTAINERS.md. Do not represent this founding appointment as a community election.

The initial mandate lasts six months. Before it expires, publish a review and a proposal for succession, representation, terms and removal. Record any vacancy and appoint a replacement through a public proposal rather than silently granting rules authority.

## Decisions

Anyone may propose a change. Likes and polls identify interest, not proof of balance.

During setup, repository administrators may merge organisational scaffolding and corrections. They may not describe a patch as council-approved until an appointed council has ratified the process.

For the three-member council, a substantive balance decision requires three affirmative votes recorded on the pull request. At least one approving maintainer must be independent of its authors. An author may explain and vote on policy, but their own work still requires independent review. Any change to council size must include ratified quorum and thresholds before approving balance changes.

Minor editorial corrections need one non-author maintainer approval and must not alter gameplay. A clarification affecting gameplay uses the substantive process.

Record the evidence, limitations, rationale and dissent. Do not count missing responses as approval. Material changes to a tested proposal require renewed review and, where relevant, renewed testing.

## Conflicts and conduct

Disclose relevant commercial, organisational and personal interests. Playing a faction does not automatically disqualify a member. A material conflict requires recusal; do not lower the approval threshold to bypass it. Appoint a temporary independent reviewer through a public appointment decision if needed.

Critique proposals, not people. Harassment, personal attacks and publication of private information are not acceptable. Maintainers explain moderation actions where doing so does not expose private information.

## Reconsideration and continuity

Reopen a decision when new evidence, an overlooked interaction or a process failure warrants it. Link the original decision and explain what changed.

Keep at least two trusted organisation owners when suitable volunteers are available. Record appointments and departures, preserve release history, and ensure no single account is the only recovery route.

Governance changes require a public proposal, at least 14 days for feedback and the substantive approval threshold after the council is appointed.
